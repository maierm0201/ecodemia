Good to know

  • Cancel any time. Monthly support ends at the end of the current month. You can cancel or change the amount or payment method yourself via “Manage your support”, or by emailing info@ecodemia.org.
  • Secure payment. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card and bank details never reach Ecodemia.
  • Invoice by email. You automatically receive an invoice for every payment. No VAT is charged under the Austrian small business scheme (Kleinunternehmerregelung).
  • Not a tax-deductible donation. Ecodemia is run as a sole proprietorship in Graz, Austria (see imprint), not as a charity. Your support is a voluntary payment to the business and is not tax-deductible. Every article stays free to read for everyone.
  • Larger amounts or partnerships? Write to us at info@ecodemia.org.
